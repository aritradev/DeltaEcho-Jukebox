## 6. Data → Sound Mapping Rules

| Variable | Input Range | Output Range / Target | Voice / Param | Label |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T2M | 10.0 – 40.0 | 220.0–880.0 Hz | sine | Temperature 10-40°C maps exponentially to pitch 220-880 Hz |
| PRECTOTCORR | 0.0 – 60.0 | 0.0–60.0 → 0.0–1.0 | rhythm_note_density | Daily rainfall 0-60 mm maps linearly to note density (0.0 to 1.0) |
| NDVI | 0.1 – 0.9 | 0.1–0.9 → 0.0–1.0 | harmonic_timbre_filter | MODIS/VIIRS NDVI 0.1-0.9 maps to sustained harmonic pad brightness |
| FIRMS_FIRES | 0 – 10 | 0–10 → 0.0–1.0 | percussion_hits | Daily active fire detections trigger sparse percussion hits |
| GRACE_EWH_CM | -20.0 – 20.0 | 55.0–110.0 Hz | bass_drone | GRACE water storage anomaly (-20 to +20 cm) maps to sub-bass drone pitch & gain |

### Notes

- **Time span covered:** two representative full calendar years, **1985** and **2025** (365 days each, Jan 1 → Dec 31), per region — a 40-year climate comparison window.
- **Monthly climatology** covers all 12 months (Jan–Dec) for both 1985 and 2025.
- The `nasa_power_direct_api` field on each region points to a live NASA POWER daily endpoint spanning `start=19850101` → `end=20251231` (the full 1985–2025 range).
- All 7 regions share an identical schema and identical variable list.
