Frames are 256×256 satellite crops measured in the browser at load time. The Cox's Bazar strip steps monthly from March 2000 to December 2010; the two NASA EIC strips step annually from 2000 to 2026. Exact acquisition days are not recorded in the filmstrip metadata. Missing weather readings use synthesis defaults; they are not measured zeroes. GISTEMP supplies a separate global anomaly layer, not the imagery. No sound is pre-recorded.
